# Identifying Potentially Undervalued MLB Hitters | 2026

## Baseball Operations & Player Evaluation Analytics

This project analyzes MLB Statcast-derived hitting data from 2024–2026 to identify players whose underlying offensive performance may be stronger than their actual results suggest.

## Tools
Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook

## Methodology
- Compare wOBA and xwOBA
- Calculate expected-performance gaps
- Evaluate barrel rate and hard-hit rate
- Analyze historical performance trends
- Develop exploratory player rankings
- Produce individual scouting reports

## Finalists
1. Brandon Nimmo
2. Heliot Ramos
3. Masyn Winn
4. Shea Langeliers
5. William Contreras

## Key Findings
Heliot Ramos demonstrated meaningful improvement in barrel rate, while Brandon Nimmo recorded the largest positive xwOBA gap among the five finalists.

## Baseball Operations Applications
Player identification, pro scouting, offensive player development, and preliminary acquisition research.

## Limitations
Positive xwOBA gaps do not guarantee future improvement. The custom scoring framework is exploratory rather than a validated predictive model. Contract values and acquisition costs were not evaluated.
